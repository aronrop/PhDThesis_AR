\subsection{Matrix-Assisted Laser/Desorption/Ionization Time-of-Flight Mass Spectrometry (MALDI-TOF MS)}
\nomenclature[z-maldiTof]{MALDI-TOF}{Matrix-Assisted Laser Desorption/Ionization Time-of-Flight}
Mass spectrometric measurements were carried out using an Autoflex Speed MALDI-TOF mass spectrometer (Bruker Daltonik, Bremen, Germany). Linear mode was applied, where the voltages of ion source 1 and ion source 2 were 19.5 kV and 18.3 kV, respectively. The instrument was equipped with a solid-state laser (355 nm). All mass spectra were externally calibrated with PNIPAM homopolymers. The matrix and ionizing agent were Trans-2-[3-(4-tert-Butylphenyl)-2-methyl-2-propenylidene]malononitrile (DCTB) and \ce{CsTFA}, respectively. Methanol was used a solvent for samples, matrix and cationizing agent. 

The final concentrations of the matrix, samples and cationizing agent were 20, 10 and 2.5mg/ml respectively. The matrix, sample and cationizing agent were mixed in a volumetric ratio of 20:4:1 and 0.5µL of the mixture was  deposited onto the MALDI target plate and allowed to air-dry prior to analysis.


\subsection{Nuclear magnetic Resonance}
\nomenclature[z-nmr]{NMR}{Nuclear Magnetic Resonance}Proton nuclear magnetic resonance (\ce{^1H}-NMR) spectra were recorded on a Bruker Avance NEO 700 MHz spectrometer equipped with a 5 mm z-gradient CryoProbe TCI. One-dimensional 1H-NMR experiments were conducted at 298 K using a \ang{45} excitation pulse, a relaxation delay of 10s, a spectral width of 13.97ppm, and 32k data points.

Spectral processing and integration were performed using Bruker TopSpin 4.0.9 and Mestrelab MestreNova 14.2 software. For each measurement, 6 mg of the sample was dissolved in 600$\mu$L of deuterated chloroform (\ce{CDCl3}).
\subsection{UV-visible Spectroscopy}
\nomenclature[z-Tcp]{\Tcp}{Cloud Point Temparature}
The cloud point temperature (\Tcp) was measured on JASCO V-750 equipped with a water-cooled Peltier-thermostated six-position autosampler (JASCO PAC-743R) with individual temperature probes. The parameters for the study were set as follows: Optical path length =1.0cm, $\lambda$=500nm, heating/cooling rate of 0.3\degc/min. Measurements were performed using polystyrene cuvettes and a polymer concentration of 10mg/ml. Temperature-dependent transmittance data were collected at approximately 0.2\degc{} interval.
Nitrogen/dry gas was used during low temperature measurements to prevent extreme condensation around the cuvettes.
